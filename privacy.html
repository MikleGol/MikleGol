Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

This policy describes how Simulation Content Engine (the "Application"), an independently operated live-stream automation tool, accesses, uses, and stores information when connected to a Google/YouTube account.

1. Application and operator

The Application is used to automate the operator's country-flag game live streams. It is primarily a private creator tool, not a public sign-in service. Questions or data requests can be directed to the operator at github.com/MikleGol.

2. Information accessed through Google and YouTube

  • Account authorization: Google OAuth access and refresh tokens for accounts explicitly connected by an account owner.
  • Channel and broadcast data: identifiers and operational metadata for the connected YouTube channel, live broadcasts, streams, videos, titles, descriptions, status, and thumbnails as needed to operate broadcasts.
  • Live chat data: chat messages, display names, identifiers, message timestamps, and relevant participation events, when interactive chat features are enabled.
  • Operational diagnostics: API responses, errors, request status, and local stream state necessary to monitor and resume the automation.

The Google OAuth permission used by the Application may be broader than any individual operation. The Application accesses YouTube data only to perform its described automation functions.

3. How information is used

Connected-account data is used to create, configure, start, monitor, and complete live streams; optionally set thumbnails or other stream metadata; read chat to recognize game-related interactions; optionally send operator-configured chat responses; and recover operations after interrupted network or API sessions. It is not used to build advertising profiles or sold to data brokers.

4. Storage, security, and retention

OAuth credentials and tokens are kept outside the public source repository on devices or servers controlled by the operator, with restricted file access. The Application may keep local operational records such as broadcast identifiers, chat message identifiers, checkpoints, and logs to support reconnects, prevent duplicate processing, and troubleshoot errors. Records are retained while operationally needed or until removed by the operator. No internet-connected system can be guaranteed perfectly secure.

5. Sharing and third parties

The Application communicates with Google and YouTube API services to perform authorized actions. Infrastructure hosting providers may process information incidentally as part of operating the server. The operator does not sell Google user data or share it with advertisers. No third-party analytics or advertising trackers are used on this informational website.

6. Viewer participation

Viewers are not required to authorize their Google account with this Application. If a viewer participates in a YouTube live chat, public chat information may be read and used by the game to process messages and show participation-related feedback. The live chat itself is governed by YouTube's own policies and settings.

7. Control, revocation, and deletion

A connected account owner may revoke the Application's Google access at any time through Google Account connections. Revocation blocks future authorized access, but may not automatically delete already stored local records. For removal of locally held tokens or operational data, contact the operator through their GitHub profile.

8. Google and YouTube terms

This Application uses YouTube API Services. Use of YouTube is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy. Google Account permissions can be reviewed in the Google Account connections settings.

9. Policy changes

This policy may be updated if the Application's features, data handling, or applicable requirements change. The updated policy and revision date will be published at this URL.
